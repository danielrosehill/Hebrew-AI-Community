# Scope: Hebrew AI Community

Part of the [Hebrew AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI) index. This list covers the **people and organisations** improving support for modern Hebrew across AI tasks, wherever they are based.

## In scope

- Research labs and academic groups publishing Hebrew-language models, datasets or benchmarks.
- Non-profits, community projects and open-source efforts focused on Hebrew AI (corpora, ASR, TTS, nikud, leaderboards).
- Companies whose product or published work is specifically Hebrew-language AI (a Hebrew-native assistant, a Hebrew ASR engine), not companies that only serve Hebrew among many languages.
- Individuals with a body of public Hebrew-language AI work (models, datasets, tools, papers).
- Communities and groups whose topic is Hebrew-language AI.

## Inclusion test

A person or organisation is listed for their **Hebrew-language AI work**, which the entry links to. Being an AI person in Israel is not enough; being outside Israel is no obstacle.

## Out of scope

- **Israeli AI in general.** Israel is the only country where modern Hebrew is an official language, so it is the natural centre of activity, and many entries here are Israeli. But an AI company, project or person that just happens to be based in Israel is out of scope: that list is far too large, and this set is about Hebrew-language AI specifically. Israel-focused AI is indexed separately in [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI).
- Biblical, rabbinic or Paleo-Hebrew work is in scope only where it is AI work on the language (a model, dataset or tool), not general Jewish-studies software.
- Personal details beyond what the person publishes on their own public profiles (Hugging Face, GitHub, website).
